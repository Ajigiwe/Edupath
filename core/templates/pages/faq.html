Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about EduPath Ghana.

Is EduPath free?
Yes — the core features (Find My Path, Find Schools, and basic Career Outcomes) are completely free and don't require an account. Premium features like unlimited past questions, detailed analytics, and termly progress analysis are available on the paid Premium plan.
How accurate are the recommendations?
Our recommendations use real admission cutoff data from Ghanaian universities. The fit algorithm considers your subjects, grades, interests, and admission requirements to generate a personalised score.
Which universities are covered?
Over 23 institutions including University of Ghana, KNUST, UCC, UDS, UEW, and technical universities nationwide.
How do I upgrade my plan?
Go to My Plan in your account menu or visit the Pricing page. You can switch plans anytime via Paystack — your new features unlock immediately after payment.
How do I contact support?
WhatsApp us at +233 599 213 810, email support@edupathghana.com, or call us during business hours (Mon–Fri, 8 AM–5 PM GMT).
Can I cancel my subscription?
Yes — you can cancel anytime from your My Plan page. Your access remains active until the end of the current billing period. We don't offer refunds for partial months unless required by law.
